# Story 4.1 — Página de vendas do Planner Água (piloto do molde)

**Status:** Live — https://plataforma.jayaroberta.com.br/planners/agua (approved by Jaya 2026-09-28)
**Epic:** 4 — Páginas de venda dos planners
**Handoff:** [../HANDOFF.md](../HANDOFF.md)

## Story

**As a** woman arriving from Instagram or from the hub who is curious about a planner,
**I want** a page that shows what the 30 days actually contain before I pay,
**so that** I buy knowing what I get, instead of landing straight on a Hotmart checkout.

## Decisions (approved by Jaya, 2026-09-28)

- Pages live in this hub at `plataforma.jayaroberta.com.br/planners/<elemento>` (canonical).
- `shaktijaya.com.br/planners/<elemento>` **redirects** to the hub URL (no proxy, no CSP change on the Shakti site).
- Visual: SAND light base + a night band (navy and gold, like the planner covers) at the opening.
- One template + one data entry per planner. Água is the pilot; the other six follow in Story 4.2 once this is approved.
- Checkout stays on Hotmart (R$57, up to 6x). Pixel IDs are configured in Hotmart and not exposed on the checkout; Jaya supplies them.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `/planners/agua` renders from a data entry in `src/data/planners.ts`; adding a planner means adding an entry and its images, no template change.
2. Sections, in order: opening (cover mockup, promise, price, CTA) · signs of the element out of balance · how it works (15–30 min a day, 4 weeks, as a couple) · the 4 weeks with their named exercises · preview of real interior pages · author · guarantee (7 days) · planner-specific FAQ · the other elements + quiz · final CTA.
3. Every sales claim traces to the planner PDF or to the existing Hotmart page (no invented bonuses, testimonials, page counts or results).
4. Images come from the real planner: cover + 3 interior pages rendered from the PDF by a script (`scripts/build-planner-art.py`); sources stay outside git, outputs versioned in `src/assets/planners/`.
5. Every CTA goes to the planner's Hotmart checkout, forwarding the visitor's `utm_*`/`src` parameters (with hub defaults when absent).
6. Meta Pixel `PageView` + `ViewContent` on the page and `InitiateCheckout` on CTA click, only when a pixel ID is configured for that planner; no pixel code otherwise.
7. The hub's Água medallion opens the sales page (same tab); the other six keep going to their checkouts until 4.2.
8. `shaktijaya.com.br/planners/agua` answers 307 to the hub URL (temporary on purpose: a permanent redirect is cached by browsers and cannot be undone if the pages move). Prepared in `Jaya_Hub_Page` branch `feat/planners-redirect`.
9. Meets Story 1.1 budgets on mobile (performance ≥ 0.95, accessibility ≥ 0.95, CLS ≤ 0.05), light and dark themes, reduced motion respected.
10. Copy passes the `revisora` review (no em dash overuse, no AI-writing tics) before Jaya sees it.
