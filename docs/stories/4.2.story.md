# Story 4.2 — Páginas de venda dos outros seis planners

**Status:** InReview — all seven planner pages live 2026-09-28 (Ar, Terra, Fogo, Éter, Despertar, Harmonia; Água from 4.1); redirects for all seven slugs; awaiting QA gate
**Epic:** 4 — Páginas de venda dos planners
**Depends on:** [4.1](4.1.story.md) (template, art script, pixel, redirect pattern)

## Story

**As a** visitor choosing a planner in the hub, on Instagram or on shaktijaya.com.br,
**I want** every planner to have the same kind of page the Água planner has,
**so that** I can see what any of the seven journeys contains before I pay.

## Decisions (Jaya, 2026-09-28)

- Same template, same visual (SAND + night opening + night weeks with gold and lit featured days).
- Order: Terra, Ar, Fogo, Éter, Despertar, Harmonia.
- Pixel `DEFAULT_PIXEL_ID` for all.
- `shaktijaya.com.br/planners/<slug>` redirects (307) to the hub page, like Água.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `/planners/{terra,ar,fogo,eter,despertar,harmonia}` render from new entries in `src/data/planners.ts`, with no template change beyond what the content needs.
2. Every sentence traces to that planner's PDF (re-spaced where glued) or to facts already used in 4.1 (price, guarantee, Hotmart access, author bio). No invented exercises, results or testimonials.
3. Preview images come only from clean pages (no glued words). Known defects: Terra has one glued phrase ("realinharoelementoTerra"), Fogo one ("intimidadepaixão").
4. All seven hub medallions open their sales page; the "other planners" row on every page links internally.
5. `shaktijaya.com.br/planners/<slug>` answers 307 for all seven slugs.
6. Copy passes `revisora`; mobile Lighthouse on each page: performance ≥ 0.95, accessibility ≥ 0.95, CLS ≤ 0.05.
7. Pixel events verified on at least one of the new pages in production.

## Change of approach (2026-09-28)

Jaya approved a dedicated page model (her Água package) over the generic template. Each planner gets its own `src/pages/planners/<slug>.astro` built from `ar.astro`, with copy written only from its final PDF. See HANDOFF "Páginas de venda dos planners".
